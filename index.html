  • Two-Stage Rocket: Pneumatic Separation System

    Pneumatic Systems Designer · Cercle Aérospatial CentraleSupélec · 2024–2025

    I led the design of the two-stage pneumatic separation system: pressure budget, valve and actuator sizing, SolidWorks CAD, and integration under mass and volume constraints.

    I built COMSOL models of pressure, airflow and thermal behavior and validated them with physical tests.

    Our team won Best Experimental Rocket at CNES C’Space 2025, with a fully successful two-stage flight (apogee 1,223 m / 4,010 ft, separation at 5.5 s) and nominal parachute recovery.

  • Real-Time General-Relativistic Black Hole Simulation

    Personal project · 2025
