Berkeley M.Eng. Capstone #161: Design-Build-Fly

Electrical Systems & Procurement Lead · Sponsored by Celedon Solutions · 2026–present

Overview

This is Berkeley M.Eng. Capstone #161, Design-Build-Fly, sponsored by Celedon Solutions. Our 4-person team is designing a hybrid lift + cruise VTOL UAV on a fixed $2,000 hardware budget.

My role

As Electrical Systems & Procurement Lead, I own the electrical architecture (power and signal distribution, wiring, test planning) and the team’s procurement and inventory system (budget tracking, vendor orders, kitting).

Status

We are in the early design phase, and a flight test is planned for April 2027. Details are limited while the project is in progress.

Workflow

We work in an AI-driven engineering workflow (Celedon’s DaVinci platform + AI agents).
