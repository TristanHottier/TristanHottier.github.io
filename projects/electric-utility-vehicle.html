Electric Utility Vehicle

Family project · 2018

My father and I designed and built this small electric utility vehicle from scratch in 2018. It has covered 1,600+ km in regular use.

The project

We built it from scratch for personal use.

What we did

  • Drew the full vehicle in CAD.
  • Defined the power system.
  • Machined parts from raw stock.
  • Welded the frame (I did that part).

Specifications and use

Top speed is about 50 km/h. The bed is 1.2 m × 0.8 m, it carries 5+ people, and it runs on a lithium battery.

It has covered 1,600+ km in regular use.
