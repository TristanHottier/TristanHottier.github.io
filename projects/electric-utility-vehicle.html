Electric Utility Vehicle

Family project · 2018

Overview

This was a family project in 2018: I designed and built it from scratch with my father. The work included CAD, the power system, and parts machined from raw stock.

Results

It has been in regular use for 1,600+ km. Top speed is about 50 km/h, it carries 5+ people, and it uses a lithium battery.
