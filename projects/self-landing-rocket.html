Self-Landing Rocket Prototype

Mechanical Team Leader · Cercle Aérospatial CentraleSupélec · 2025–2026

Overview

This is the self-landing rocket prototype of the Cercle Aérospatial CentraleSupélec (2025–2026): a thrust-vector-control (TVC) prototype with an orientable nozzle.

My role

As Mechanical Team Leader, I led the mechanical design and build of the TVC prototype.

Status

We built and integrated the flight hardware within one semester. The software was not ready, so we never launched the prototype.

The images on this page are CAD models.
