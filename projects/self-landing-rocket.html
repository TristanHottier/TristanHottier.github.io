Icarus: Self-Landing Rocket Prototype

Mechanical Team Leader · Cercle Aérospatial CentraleSupélec · 2025–2026

Icarus is a thrust-vector-control rocket prototype with an orientable nozzle, built by the Cercle Aérospatial CentraleSupélec. I was Mechanical Team Leader. We built the hardware in one semester, but the software was not ready, so it was never launched.

The project

The prototype steers with thrust vector control: an orientable nozzle changes the direction of the thrust. We had one semester to design, build and integrate it.

What I did

As Mechanical Team Leader, I led the mechanical design and build of the TVC prototype.

Status

We built and integrated the flight hardware within one semester. The software was not ready, so we never launched the prototype.
