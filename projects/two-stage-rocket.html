Two-Stage Rocket: Pneumatic Separation System

Pneumatic Systems Designer · Cercle Aérospatial CentraleSupélec · 2024–2025

Overview

This is the two-stage rocket of the Cercle Aérospatial CentraleSupélec (2024–2025). This page covers its pneumatic separation system, my part of the project.

My role

As Pneumatic Systems Designer, I led the design of the two-stage pneumatic separation system: pressure budget, valve and actuator sizing, SolidWorks CAD, and integration under mass and volume constraints.

I built COMSOL models of pressure, airflow and thermal behavior and validated them with physical tests, for example leak rates and actuator response time.

Team results

Our team won Best Experimental Rocket at CNES C’Space 2025.

Our rocket made a fully successful two-stage flight, reaching 145 m/s (324 mph) and an apogee of 1,223 m (4,010 ft). Separation happened at 5.5 s and the parachute recovery was nominal.

Impulse was 3,319 N·s for the first stage and 137 N·s for the second stage.
