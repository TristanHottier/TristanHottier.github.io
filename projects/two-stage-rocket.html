Pegasus: Two-Stage Rocket

Pneumatic Systems Designer · Cercle Aérospatial CentraleSupélec · 2024–2025

I led the design of the pneumatic separation system for Pegasus, the two-stage rocket of the Cercle Aérospatial CentraleSupélec. Our team won Best Experimental Rocket at CNES C’Space 2025.

The problem

A two-stage rocket has to separate its stages in flight. The separation system had to fit inside the rocket’s mass and volume limits.

What I designed

I led the design of the pneumatic separation system. It covers:

  • the pressure budget
  • valve and actuator sizing
  • SolidWorks CAD
  • integration into the rocket under the mass and volume constraints

How I checked it

I built COMSOL models of pressure, airflow and thermal behavior, and validated them with physical tests such as leak rates and actuator response time.

Team results

Our team won Best Experimental Rocket at CNES C’Space 2025. The flight was fully successful: clean separation and a safe descent under parachute.

The rocket reached an apogee of 1,223 m (4,010 ft) and 145 m/s (324 mph). Separation happened at 5.5 s and the parachute recovery was nominal. The motors delivered 3,319 N·s of impulse on the first stage and 137 N·s on the second.
